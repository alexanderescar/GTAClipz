# Permission outreach

Send this the day you start. ~20–30% say yes. Smaller creators (5k–80k
followers) say yes far more often than the big ones, and non-English
creators on the launch roster almost always do — you're giving them
English-language reach they can't buy.

Keep it short. Nobody reads a pitch during launch week.

---

**Subject / DM opener:** `Clipping your GTA 6 streams — ok with you?`

> Hey {name} — I run a GTA 6 clips account ({handle}).
>
> I'd like to clip your launch streams for Shorts/TikTok/Reels. Every post
> credits you by name in the caption, tags you, and links your channel in
> the first comment and my bio. I don't post anything spoiler-y, and I'll
> delete anything you ask me to, no questions.
>
> Fine by you? A yes here is all I need.
>
> — {your name}

---

**If they run a paid clipping program instead**, join that instead of asking.
The brief is a cleaner license than a DM, and it pays.

**Log the yes.** Screenshot it, drop it in `docs/permissions/<streamer>.png`,
then set the source's `permission: granted` and `evidence:` in
`config/sources.yml`. Future-you needs to prove this existed.

**Re-check `published` rules monthly.** Creators change their clip policies,
usually right after someone abuses them.

## Where to look first (launch week)

| Source | Why |
|---|---|
| contentrewards.com | Paid campaigns, no application. Budgets get claimed in minutes — check daily. |
| whop.com/discover/content-rewards | Same marketplace, creator-funded campaigns |
| Streamer Discords, #clips channel | Many post blanket clip rules there and nowhere else |
| Channel "About"/panels on Twitch & Kick | Published clip policies live here |
| YouTube × Rockstar launch roster | 31 named creators, all reachable, all new to GTA 6 clipping |
