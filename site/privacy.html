Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

GTAClipz ("the app") is a private publishing tool used by its operator to post video clips to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts (@gtavicecityclipzz). It is not offered to other users.

Information the app accesses

  • Account access tokens for the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, used solely to upload videos to those accounts. Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.
  • Public stream data from Twitch and YouTube (public clip listings, public video, public chat replays), used to find and prepare highlight clips.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store or sell personal information about viewers, followers or any other individuals. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Google user data

The app requests only the youtube.upload scope, to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google user data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Rendered video files are stored temporarily (normally no more than 3 days) to complete uploads, then deleted.

Removal requests

Creators whose content appears in a clip can request removal at any time by emailing gtavicecityclipzzz@gmail.com.
